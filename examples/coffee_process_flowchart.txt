A beautiful illustrated flowchart showing the journey of coffee from farm to cup.

Steps (left to right or top to bottom):
1. Coffee cherries on a plant (green/red berries)
2. Harvesting (hands picking)
3. Processing (washing/drying)
4. Roasting (beans turning brown)
5. Grinding
6. Brewing
7. A steaming cup of coffee

Connect each step with curved arrows. Each step should be in a rounded card with a small illustration and label. Use warm earth tones: browns, greens, cream. Add a subtle coffee-stain watercolor texture in the background.

Style: Flat illustration, warm and inviting, suitable for a cafe wall or food blog.
